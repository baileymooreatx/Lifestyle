# Fried Foods

Fried foods are unhealthy for several interconnected reasons. Frying 
transforms otherwise reasonable foods into calorie-dense, pro-inflammatory, 
cholesterol-worsening items that carry measurable risks for heart disease, 
diabetes, obesity, and possibly cancer.

## High Caloric Density

Food absorbs oil during frying, substantially boosting calories. A baked potato
has ~93 calories; the same potato deep-fried into fries jumps to ~312 calories
and 15 g of fat.

## Obesity

Beyond sheer calories, trans fats may disrupt hormones that regulate appetite
and fat storage, compounding weight gain.

## Trans Fats and Oxidized Oils

High-temperature frying converts healthy unsaturated fats into trans fats and
oxidized byproducts (like hydroxylinoleate and 4-hydroxynonenal). Trans fats
raise LDL ("bad") cholesterol, lower HDL ("good") cholesterol, and promote
systemic inflammation. Reused restaurant oils make this worse.

## Heart Disease Risk

A 2021 meta-analysis in *Heart* (750,000+ people) found that eating the most
fried foods weekly was associated with a **28% higher risk** of major
cardiovascular events (heart attack, stroke, heart failure) compared to eating
the least.

## Inflammation and Oxidative Stress

Frying produces advanced glycation end products (AGEs) and free radicals that
damage cells, blood vessels, and DNA over time.

## Acrylamide

A potentially carcinogenic compound forms when starch-rich foods (like potatoes)
are cooked above ~350°F (175°C).

## High Sodium

Batters and seasonings add significant salt, contributing to high blood
pressure.

## Gut Microbiome Disruption

High-fat fried foods reduce the diversity of beneficial gut bacteria, which is
linked to increased risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, and cardiometabolic
disease.

## Digestive Strain

The absorbed fat is harder to digest, commonly causing gas, bloating, stomach
pain, and diarrhea.
