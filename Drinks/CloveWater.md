# Clove Water

A YouTube [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBVF-nEl2z0), presented by
*Ben Azadi*, explores the health benefits of drinking **clove water** before bed
to improve metabolic health and sleep quality. Clove is highlighted for its
incredibly high antioxidant capacity, specifically its content of **eugenol**,
which helps neutralize free radicals more effectively than many well-known
superfoods.

YouTube [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XEcssz8NOhg) by *Dr. Eric Berg* 
explains the **health benefits of cloves** and demonstrates how to prepare 
**clove water** to consume at night. Historically prized for their value, cloves
offer numerous medicinal properties, including **antibacterial, antifungal, and
antiviral effects**.

## Key Health Benefits and Mechanisms

* **Blood Sugar Management:** Scientific studies mentioned suggest that clove
  polyphenol extracts can improve fasting blood sugar, insulin resistance, and
  A1C levels by enhancing muscle glucose uptake and inhibiting liver glucose
  production.
* **Overnight Metabolic Support:** Drinking clove water before bed targets the
  overnight window when the liver often performs excessive glucose output,
  helping to stabilize blood sugar while sleeping.
* **Inflammation and Sleep:** Clove compounds influence the *NF-kappa B* pathway
  to reduce inflammation and modulate *GABA* receptors, which can assist in
  calming the mind for better sleep.
* **Antioxidant Activation:** Clove intake supports the recycling of
  *glutathione*, the body's master antioxidant, and activates *NRF2*.
* **Oral Health:** Clove oil acts as a natural analgesic for toothaches, often
  outperforming *benzocaine* in speed and longevity of relief. It is effective
  against gingivitis and plaque-forming biofilms.
* **Skin Inflammation:** *Eugenol*, a key component in cloves, can help
  reduce skin swelling and redness.
* **Detoxification:** Stimulates liver enzymes.
* **Digestion:** Reduces bloating and gas.
* **Respiratory Health:** Helps with bronchitis, coughs, and mucus.
* **Immune & Heart Health:** Supports circulation and overall immune
  function.

## Preparation

1. Use **4 to 5 whole cloves** (always whole to prevent oxidation).
2. Bring **one cup of water** to a boil, then simmer with a lid on for 5
   minutes.
3. Let it steep for 10 minutes (keep the lid on at all times to prevent the
   volatile eugenol from evaporating).
4. Consume roughly **30 minutes before bed**. You can add a little raw honey if
   the taste is too bitter.

## Safety Considerations

* **Medication Interactions:** Individuals taking **blood thinners** should
  consult their doctor because the active compound, eugenol, has anti-platelet
  activity. Additionally, those on **diabetes medications** should be cautious,
  as the clove water may cause glucose levels to drop lower than intended due to
  the additive effects of the spice.
* **Surgery:** If you have surgery scheduled, the speaker advises stopping the
  consumption of clove water at least **two weeks prior** to the procedure.
* **Avoid Clove Oil:** Ben Azadi emphasizes that one should **never drink
  concentrated clove oil**, as it is highly potent and can cause significant
  liver damage. The protocol is strictly for using whole clove buds in water.
