## Essential Amino Acids

[12 Plant Foods With All 9 Essential Amino Acids](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3f9GVC5qB0E)

This video from **The Homestead Gardener** challenges the common belief that
complete protein (containing all nine essential amino acids) can only be 
found in animal products like meat, eggs, or dairy. Jack explores 12 plant-based 
foods, some of which may already be in your pantry or garden, that provide 
these essential amino acids.  

The 12 Plant-Based Protein Sources:  

1. **Potatoes:** A humble vegetable where protein quality is high, even
   if the amount is modest (4g per potato). Keeping the skin on and using proper
   storage is recommended.
2. **[Pistachios](../Nuts/Pistachio.md):** An ancient nut confirmed by research
   to be a complete protein source, offering about 6g per ounce.
3. **[Chia Seeds](../Seeds/Chia.md):** Aztec-era seeds that provide protein, 
   fiber, and omega-3s; they should be soaked in liquid before consumption.
4. **Buckwheat:** Despite the name, it is a gluten-free seed related to
   rhubarb, providing a balanced amino acid profile including high lysine.
5. **[Quinoa](../Seeds/Quinoa.md):** An Andean crop that is a complete protein;
   it is best to rinse the bitter *saponin* coating before cooking.
6. **[Hemp Seeds](../Seeds/HempHearts.md):** These provide roughly 10g of
   protein per 3 tablespoons and are rich in healthy fats; store in the 
   fridge to prevent them from going rancid.
7. **Wild Rice:** A native North American aquatic grass that offers more
   protein and lysine than conventional rice.
8. **Amaranth:** A sacred seed of the Aztecs that is rich in lysine and can be 
   popped like tiny popcorn or used in porridge.
9. **Teff:** The tiny grain behind Ethiopian *injera*; it is high in iron and 
   fiber and cooks very quickly.
10. **Moringa:** Known as the "miracle tree," its leaves contain all nine 
    essential amino acids and can be dried into a nutrient-dense powder.
11. **Water Lentils:** The world's smallest flowering plants, these grow
    extremely fast and are highly nutritious when grown in clean, controlled
    water.
12. **Soybeans:** One of the most protein-dense plant foods (30g per cup
    cooked), used in many forms like *tofu*, *tempeh*, and *edamame*.

Jack emphasizes that understanding food history and proper preparation is key to
utilizing these sources effectively without relying on modern supplements.
