# Basil

Basil (*Ocimum basilicum*) is a low-calorie herb whose value comes from its
vitamin, mineral, and antioxidant content rather than macronutrients. Its main
benefits are:

- **Vitamin K** – the standout nutrient, supporting blood clotting and bone
  health.
- **Vitamin A / carotenoids** (β-carotene, lutein, zeaxanthin) – support vision,
  skin, and immune function.
- **Antioxidants** (rosmarinic acid, eugenol, flavonoids) – help combat
  oxidative stress and may reduce inflammation.
- **Minerals** (potassium, magnesium, calcium, iron, manganese) – support
  heart/blood-pressure regulation, muscle and nerve function, and red blood cell
  formation.

Because typical servings are small (a few leaves), the per‑100‑g figures look
high but a normal portion contributes only a modest amount.
