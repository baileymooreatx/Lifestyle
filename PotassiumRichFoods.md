# Potassium Rich Foods

Potassium is an essential mineral that supports heart, nerve, and muscle
function. While bananas are famously associated with potassium, many other foods
contain significantly higher amounts per serving. The current Daily Value (DV)
for potassium is **4,700 mg**.

## Key Food Groups

### Vegetables and Tubers  

Root vegetables and leafy greens are among the most potent sources. **Potatoes**, 
**sweet potatoes**, and **yams** provide substantial potassium, especially
when baked with the skin. Dark leafy greens like **spinach**, **Swiss chard**,
and **beet greens** offer extremely high concentrations when cooked. Winter
squashes, such as **acorn squash** and **pumpkin**, are also excellent choices.

### Pulses    

Beans and [lentils](./Pulses/Lentil.md) are nutrient-dense powerhouses. **White 
beans**, **kidney beans**, **lima beans**, and **lentils** consistently rank at 
the top of potassium lists. A single cup of cooked lentils or white beans can 
provide over 15% of the daily recommended value.

### Fruits  

While **bananas** are the most well-known source, dried fruits often contain
more potassium per serving due to concentrated nutrients. **Dried apricots**, 
**prunes**, **raisins**, and **dates** are exceptionally rich. Fresh fruits like
**avocados**, **oranges**, **cantaloupe**, and **mangoes** are also significant
contributors.

### Protein Sources  

Animal proteins contribute meaningfully to potassium intake. **Fish**
(particularly salmon, tuna, and halibut), **chicken**, **beef**, and
**pork** contain moderate to high levels. Dairy products like **milk** and 
**yogurt** are also reliable sources. For plant-based protein, **soybeans** and 
**tofu** are excellent options.

### Other Sources  

Certain beverages and additives are rich in potassium. **Orange juice**, 
**tomato juice**, **coconut water**, and even **coffee** contribute to daily
intake. **Salt substitutes** often use potassium chloride instead of sodium
chloride, making them very high in potassium, which is important for individuals
monitoring sodium intake but potentially dangerous for those with kidney issues.
