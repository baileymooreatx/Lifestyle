# Protein

Provided by Ben Azadi's YouTube video
[This Food Melts Dangerous Belly Fat Faster Than Any Other](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUdZbspetKw).  

This video explores how **dietary protein** can naturally support weight loss,
improve metabolic health, and reduce cravings by leveraging the body's own
satiety hormones. Ben Azadi explains that **protein** functions
similarly to *GLP-1* medications by stimulating natural hormonal signals that
decrease hunger, yet it also provides the critical benefit of **preserving
muscle mass**.

## Key Takeaways

* **Protein Leverage:** The body seeks to meet a specific protein target. If
  protein intake is low, the body may continue to crave food to hit that target,
  leading to overeating.
* **Visceral Fat:** Unlike subcutaneous fat, visceral fat surrounds vital organs
  and is highly active metabolically. Increasing protein intake has been shown
  in studies to help specifically target and reduce this dangerous fat.
* **Muscle Preservation:** Maintaining muscle is crucial for insulin sensitivity
  and a healthy metabolic rate. A high-protein approach helps protect lean mass
  while shedding fat.

## The Protocol

1. **Breakfast:** Consume **30 to 40 grams of protein** at your first meal.
2. **Order of Intake:** Eat protein and vegetables first, and carbohydrates last
   to dampen glucose spikes.
3. **Pre-meal:** For the meal where you tend to overeat (usually dinner),
   consume **25 grams of protein** 20 to 30 minutes beforehand.
4. **Daily Target:** Aim for approximately **1 gram of protein per pound** of
   your ideal body weight.

## Best Protein Sources

The video ranks five of the best protein-rich foods to help suppress hunger and
reduce visceral fat:

1. **[Whole Eggs](./Egg.md):** Ranked number one, these are highly effective for 
   satiety and weight management.
2. **Shellfish:** Including shrimp, scallops, and oysters, these offer a very
  high protein-to-calorie ratio.
3. **Wild Caught Salmon and Sardines:** These provide protein along with
  beneficial anti-inflammatory omega-3 fats.
4. **Grass-fed Beef:** This source is noted for having a high density of leucine,
  an amino acid essential for muscle protein synthesis.
5. **Plain Greek Yogurt or Cottage Cheese:** Dairy proteins digest slowly, which
  helps maintain fullness for several hours. Yogurt **must be plain**, not 
  flavored. Flavored varieties contain large amounts of sugar.
