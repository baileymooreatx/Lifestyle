# 5-HTP

5-HTP (5-hydroxytryptophan) is a precursor to **serotonin**, so its effects
center on raising brain serotonin levels. Evidence is generally preliminary.

## Potential Benefits

**Potential benefits (mixed evidence):**

- **Mood/depression** – Some small trials found it comparable to certain
  antidepressants; most evidence is weak or conflicting.
- **Sleep** – May improve sleep latency and REM sleep by boosting melatonin
  production.
- **Appetite/weight** – May increase satiety and reduce carb cravings, leading
  to modest weight loss in some studies.
- **Anxiety** – Limited evidence of benefit, though higher doses (≥225 mg) may
  *worsen* anxiety in some people.
- **Fibromyalgia** – 100 mg, 3×/day for 1–3 months may reduce pain, stiffness,
  and fatigue.
- **Migraine** – One older study found 71% reduction in attacks, but other
  studies show no benefit.

## Side Effects

**Common side effects:**
* Nausea
* Diarrhea
* Stomach upset
* Dizziness
* Drowsiness
* Headache
* Heartburn.

**Serious risks:**

- **Serotonin syndrome** – A potentially life-threatening condition (agitation,
  fever, rapid heart rate, muscle rigidity) that can occur when 5-HTP is
  combined with SSRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic drugs.
- **Eosinophilia-myalgia syndrome (EMS)** – A rare, serious condition linked to
  contaminated 5-HTP/tryptophan products (muscle pain, skin rash, respiratory
  issues).
- **Allergic reactions** – Hives, swelling, breathing difficulty.

**Key caveat:** 5-HTP supplements are **not FDA-regulated as drugs**, so quality
and purity vary. It should not be taken with antidepressants or other
serotonergic medications without medical supervision.

## Sleep

For sleep, the evidence-based approach is:

**Start at 50 mg**, taken **30–60 minutes before bed**. If no effect after ~2
weeks, increase by 50–100 mg increments.

**Research-backed range:** Most studies showing measurable changes in sleep (
faster onset, more REM, more slow-wave sleep) used **200 mg or more** per night.
A 2024 RCT in older adults found **100 mg** daily improved sleep latency by ~18
minutes over 8 weeks. A 200 mg dose was shown to increase REM sleep.

**Practical dosing by concern:**

| Goal                   |  Start | Research upper range |
|:-----------------------|-------:|---------------------:|
| Trouble falling asleep |  50 mg |               200 mg |
| Nighttime wakings      | 100 mg |               300 mg |
| More REM / deep sleep  | 100 mg |           200–400 mg |

**Key caveats:**

- Doses ≥200 mg are more likely to cause **vivid dreams or nightmares** and GI
  upset (nausea, diarrhea).
- Effects may take **several weeks** to plateau, and one RCT found benefits
  faded by week 12 (possible tolerance).
- As noted before, **do not combine with SSRIs, MAOIs, or other serotonergic
  drugs** without medical guidance.
- Individual response varies widely — some people report benefit at 25–50 mg,
  while others need 200 mg or more.
