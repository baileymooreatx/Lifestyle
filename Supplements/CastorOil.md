# Castor Oil

Castor oil is a vegetable oil derived from the seeds of the *Ricinus communis*
plant. Its active compound, **ricinoleic acid**, gives it anti-inflammatory,
antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. The main evidence-based medicinal
benefits follow.

## Constipation relief  

The only FDA-approved use. Taken orally, it acts as a stimulant laxative, 
producing a bowel movement within 6 to 12 hours by triggering intestinal muscle 
contractions.  

## Joint and muscle pain  

Applied topically, its anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects may ease arthritis 
and osteoarthritis pain. One study found it comparable to an NSAID for knee 
osteoarthritis pain.  


## Wound healing  

Its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties help promote tissue repair 
and reduce infection risk. It is used in medical ointments (e.g., Venelex) for 
pressure ulcers.  

## Skin Moisturizing & Acne  

Fatty acids hydrate and soften skin. Antimicrobial properties may help reduce 
acne-causing bacteria.  

## Labor Induction  

Some research suggests it may stimulate uterine contractions, though it should 
only be used under medical supervision.  

## Dry eye relief  

Some evidence supports its use in managing dry eye symptoms.

**Side effects** of oral use include abdominal cramps, diarrhea, nausea, and
dehydration. Topical use may cause allergic contact dermatitis in some
individuals. Excessive ingestion can lead to more serious GI distress.
